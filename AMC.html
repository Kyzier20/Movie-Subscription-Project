    The tiers of AMC

    AMC Stubs Insider

    Free to join, you can earn points for every dollar spent on tickets and concessions. You also get a free large popcorn on your birthday.

    AMC Stubs Premiere

    For $15 a year, you can earn points for every dollar spent on tickets and concessions. You also get a free large popcorn on your birthday, and you can skip the lines with priority lanes.

    AMC Stubs A-List

    For $19.95 a month, you can see up to 3 movies per week, earn points for every dollar spent on concessions, and you can skip the lines with priority lanes.
